Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

1. Overview

GM Academy ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website located at sommeow.github.io/gamemastersneedprivacythemost (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information. By using the Site you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

Automatically collected data. When you visit the Site, standard server logs and analytics tools may record your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages visited, and the date and time of your visit. This information is collected in aggregate and is not linked to any personally identifiable information.

Cookies and similar technologies. We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to operate the Site and, where you have consented, to analyze traffic patterns. You can control cookie preferences through the consent banner or your browser settings. Refusing certain cookies may affect Site functionality.

Information you provide. This Site does not currently include forms, accounts, or any mechanism for you to submit personal information. If that changes, this policy will be updated accordingly.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use automatically collected data solely to:

  • Operate and maintain the Site
  • Understand how visitors interact with our content
  • Detect and address technical issues
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

4. Cookies & Consent

We use a consent management platform (Osano) to give you control over non-essential cookies. When you first visit the Site, you will be presented with a cookie consent banner. You may accept all cookies, reject non-essential cookies, or customize your preferences at any time. Your choices are stored in a first-party cookie and honored on subsequent visits.

For more information about how Osano processes data on our behalf, see Osano's Privacy Policy.

5. Third-Party Services

The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect certain data as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. We have no control over GitHub's data practices.

6. Data Retention

Aggregate analytics data is retained for up to 26 months. Cookie consent preferences are retained for 12 months, after which you will be asked again. We do not store personally identifiable information beyond what is described in this policy.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of personal data we hold about you, as well as the right to data portability and the right to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these rights, please use our Data Subject Request form or contact us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

Data subject requests are intake-processed on our behalf by Osano through its data request portal (hosted at my.datasubject.com). Information you submit through the request form — such as your name, email address, and the nature of your request — is processed by Osano solely to facilitate and fulfill your request. See Osano's Privacy Policy for details.

8. Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Site after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
